Changed:

- The Default layout takes the touches and knob pushes of upstream OOS again: a push of the horizontal SCALE knob opens
  or closes the zoom, a finger held on a channel's card switches AC/DC coupling, a tap on the lower half of the TIME
  card toggles the zoom, the right half of the trigger card steps the source and the sweep, a vertical drag on the
  plot moves the selected channel, and the multipurpose knobs keep their job while the cursors are on. Minimal and
  Studio keep the fine time base on the push, the quick actions on a hold, the taps on traces and markers, the drag of
  the trace under the finger and the cursors on the knobs ([First Steps](wiki/First-Steps.md)).

Changed:

- Studio: the first view of the measurements panel is a strip of cells of one line, each with the source, the name
  and the value ("CH1 Pk-pk 633 mV"), as the measurement strip of the Default layout has them. The cells stand in
  as many columns as the panel is wide: a low panel under the plot shows a dozen measurements where the list of
  one measurement a row showed two, and a narrow panel at the side has one column. A tap on a cell opens the
  settings of its measurement. The table is the second view now and the cards are the third (host; not yet seen
  on a unit) ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md)).
- Studio: with the list of one measurement a row its gestures and its menu are gone: the swipe to the left that
  removed a row (the table still has it, with Undo), the hold and drag that moved a row, the pin of a row, and
  the menu of a row (Settings, Pin, Move up, Move down, Remove, statistics of one row, Labels on trace, About this
  measurement). The pin is on a card; a measurement is removed in the table, from the menu of its badge or on the
  Measure page; Labels on trace is in the Measure settings. Nothing moves a measurement in the list any more
  (host; not yet seen on a unit) ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md)).

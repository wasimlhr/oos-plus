Changed:

- Studio: the expand button of the measurements panel does what it does for every view. The waveform and the
  measurements become tabs of one group that fills the area, the measurements in front, and a tap on the Waveform
  tab shows the waveform; the same button, Escape or a double tap on the strip leads back. The table of all
  measurements that replaced the waveform is gone with its own buttons (its pager, Reset statistics, the pin in each
  row, and the columns chosen by Measure > Statistics): the panel has the strip, the table and the cards at full
  width, and a set-up stored with the old table open loads with the panel docked
  ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md#the-measurements-panel-of-studio)) (host; not
  yet seen on a unit).
- Studio: while the measurements panel is in front of a group of tabs its controls (source, gate, search, pin,
  statistics, add) are a line under the strip. In the strip they pushed the names of the tabs into the list behind
  the arrow.
- Studio: the cards of a wide measurements panel scroll by the columns they are drawn in. The list moved two cards
  a step whatever the number of columns, so the cards changed columns while scrolling.

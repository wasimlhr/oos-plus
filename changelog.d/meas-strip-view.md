Changed:

- Studio: the first view of the measurements panel is a strip of cells of one line, each with the source, the name
  and the value ("CH1 Pk-pk 633 mV"), as the measurement strip of the Default layout has them. The cells stand in
  as many columns as the panel is wide: a low panel under the plot shows a dozen measurements where the list of
  one measurement a row showed two, and a narrow panel at the side has one column. The right end of a cell is
  its pin, and a tap on the rest opens the settings of its measurement, which have Remove under them now (it asks
  first). The table is the second view now and the cards are the third (host; not yet seen
  on a unit) ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md)).
- Studio: with the list of one measurement a row its gestures and its menu are gone: the swipe to the left that
  removed a row (the table still has it, with Undo), the hold and drag that moved a row, and the menu of a row
  (Settings, Pin, Move up, Move down, Remove, statistics of one row, Labels on trace, About this measurement). The
  pin is on a cell and on a card; Remove is under the settings of a measurement; Labels on trace is in the Measure
  settings. Nothing moves a measurement in the list any more
  (host; not yet seen on a unit) ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md)).

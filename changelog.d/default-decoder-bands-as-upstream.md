Interface:

- Default layout: the decoders look as upstream's again. A band is the dim name ("1 UART CH1") and a box per field,
  and stays while its source channel is off; the byte that ends an I2C read shows " N"; **Decode > Event table**
  opens the table of decoder events over the plot, and the Events pane is the list of fields in one or two columns.
  Minimal and Studio keep the message boxes, the bit row and the Events pane with its views
  ([Measurements and Analysers](wiki/Measurements-and-Analysers.md)).

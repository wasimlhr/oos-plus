Added:

- `:BUS<n>:FLEXray:BAUD` sets the FlexRay bit rate (the same as `:BUS<n>:OPTion1`).
- `:CURSor:MANual:SOURce MATHn` puts the cursors on a math channel's result (a reference copy).
- `:UI:DIALog?` reads the notice that covers the screen and `:UI:DIALog:CLOSe` closes it.
- `:TRIGger:BUS:WHEN TSS` is another name of `FRAMe`, the FlexRay trigger at the end of the TSS.

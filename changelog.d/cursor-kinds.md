Changed:

- Studio: the Cursors page offers the kinds of cursors, named as the Cursor menu of the Default layout names its
  modes: Track (two cursors on the chosen trace, as before), Time (two vertical cursors) and Volt (two horizontal
  cursors). Add puts the chosen kind on; while cursors are on, a tap on another kind changes them. The horizontal
  cursors were reached through Settings alone (host; not yet seen on a unit).

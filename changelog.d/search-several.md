Added:

- Studio has up to three searches at once (Add > Search again): Search 1, 2 and 3, each with its own source, slope
  and level, its own colour and its own line of marks at the top of the plot. The count, previous and next, and the
  event list are of the selected search, which the row **Search** of the sheet chooses. Over SCPI `:SEARch2:...` and
  `:SEARch3:...` are the second and third; a command without a number is Search 1 as before
  ([guide](docs/guide.md), [SCPI](docs/scpi/search.md)).
